## **Z-library用户，你收到了吗？**

---

**Fundraising September 15, 2025 – October 1, 2025**
**筹款活动 2025 年 9 月 15 日 - 2025 年 10 月 1 日**

---

**Hello, Xingwangzhe!** 你好,**Xingwangzhe**!
Unfortunately, you haven't visited our site for several weeks, and during this time we have had many changes (we updated the design and implemented interesting new features).
很遗憾，您已经有好几周没访问我们的网站了，在这段时间里我们做了很多改动（更新了设计，还增加了不少有趣的新功能）。

---

Z-Library is one of the largest resources in the world, providing free access to unique books and scientific articles in many different languages since 2009. The main purpose of Z-Library's existence is to enhance the level of education of people around the world. Despite facing numerous challenges, we are doing everything possible to provide free access to knowledge for millions of people across the globe, and we expect you to help us with that and to support us. It is only through your active participation and support that the library is able to continue functioning and developing.
Z-Library 是全球最大的资源库之一，自 2009 年以来，一直免费提供多种语言的独特书籍和科学文章。Z-Library 存在的主要目的是提高全球人民的教育水平。尽管面临诸多挑战，我们仍在竭尽全力为全球数以百万计的人提供免费获取知识的途径，我们希望您能帮助我们并支持我们。只有通过您的积极参与和支持，图书馆才能继续运作和发展。

---

**Recently, we have introduced several important features to enhance user experience and make knowledge more accessible:**
**最近，我们引入了几个重要的功能来提升用户体验并使知识更易于获取：**

- **Official mirrors registration page**; **官方镜像注册页面**；
- **Exclusive AURORA Mode**; **专属极光模式**；
- **Redesigned book cover upload**; **重新设计的书籍封面上传**；
- **Enhanced "My Library" filters**; **增强的“我的图书馆”筛选功能**；
- **New version (3.0.0) of our desktop application**; **我们桌面应用程序的新版本（3.0.0）**；
- **Main Litera Reader updates**: mobile horizontal scrolling; two-page reading mode; social activity panel with list of user quotes; note creation function for quotes; progress bar quote marks; **主要更新内容**：移动端横向滚动；双页阅读模式；带有用户引语列表的社交活动面板；引语笔记创建功能；进度条引语标记；
- **And much more…** **还有更多……**

---

On September 15, 2025, as in March and September of each year, we launched additional fundraising to project maintenance and development. Anyone who donates during this period will receive **UNLIMITED downloads for 31 days**. The fundraising will run until October 1, 2025.
2025 年 9 月 15 日，与每年 3 月和 9 月一样，我们开启了额外的筹款活动，用于项目维护和开发。在此期间捐款的任何人都将获得 **31 天的无限次下载**。此次筹款活动将持续到 2025 年 10 月 1 日。

---

Millions of people, mostly from educational institutions, use Z-Library for their purposes. And that means we're on the right track. However, reaching our goals would be challenging without your support. Your active support gives strength to our Team and inspires to work. Each donated dollar is not only project development support, but it is also the confidence that you really need our project!
数以百万计的人，主要是来自教育机构的人，将 Z-Library 用于他们的目的。这意味着我们走在正确的道路上。然而，没有您的支持，要实现我们的目标将会很困难。您的积极支持为我们的团队注入了力量，并激励着我们工作。您捐赠的每一美元不仅是对项目发展的支持，也是您确实需要我们这个项目的信心体现。
